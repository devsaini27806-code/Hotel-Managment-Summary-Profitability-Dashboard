# 🏨 Hotel Summary & Profitability Dashboard | Excel Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Hotel Summary & Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based data analytics project designed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data visualization, KPI tracking, and profitability analysis.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance, helping users analyze revenue, gross profit, sales trends, regional performance, and sales channel contributions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and profitability.
- Track key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare sales performance across countries and regions.
- Understand revenue distribution across different sales channels.
- Support data-driven business decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| 💰 Total Revenue | $645,223 |
| 📈 Total Gross Profit | $296,384 |
| 📊 Gross Profit Margin | 45.94% |
| 📦 Total Units Sold | 6,698 |
| 🧾 Total Orders | 2,707 |

## ✨ Dashboard Features

- **Monthly Revenue & Profit Analysis:** Track monthly business performance and identify trends.
- **Top Countries by Revenue:** Identify the highest-performing countries based on revenue.
- **Revenue Share by Sales Channel:** Analyze the contribution of different sales channels to total revenue.
- **Region-wise Performance Comparison:** Compare revenue and profitability across regions.
- **Country-wise Revenue Visualization:** Explore geographical sales performance.
- **Interactive KPI Tracking:** Monitor key metrics through a consolidated dashboard.
- **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Organize and prepare raw data for reliable analysis and reporting.
